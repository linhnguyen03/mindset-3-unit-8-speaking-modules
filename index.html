In IELTS Speaking Part 1, most of the questions will be on topics you are familiar with and can talk about from personal experience. In Part 3, you will be expected to expand your answers and speak in more general terms.

Exercise 4

Read the information. Then read the IELTS Speaking Part 3 questions. Put the sentences in the correct order (1st, 2nd, 3rd) to complete the responses.

A useful way of answering IELTS Speaking Part 3 questions is to structure your response in the following way:
  • Make a statement.
  • Explain your answer.
  • Support your statement.

Exercise 5

Read the information. Then complete the responses to IELTS Speaking Part 3 questions with the phrases in the box.

Indefinite pronouns with a defining relative clause (e.g. anyone who and something that) are a good way of making general statements. They can be useful in IELTS Speaking Part 3.
